Yara Sa'di-Ibraheem, PhD

Human & Political Geography · Urbanism · Infrastructure Studies

Specialises in political geography, with research interests including indigenous geographies and temporalities, settler colonialism, neoliberal urbanism, and infrastructure, with a focus on the Middle East. Her main research explores socio-political dimensions of infrastructure in colonial settings— principally telecommunications and playgrounds—as well as neoliberal planning under settler colonialism and the spatial-temporal experiences of indigenous communities.
